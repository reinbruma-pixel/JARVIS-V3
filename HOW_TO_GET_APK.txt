1. Make a free account at github.com and create a new repository (Private is fine).
2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in EVERYTHING inside this Jarvis folder
   (including the hidden .github folder), then Commit.
3. Open the "Actions" tab -> "Build Jarvis APK" -> wait about 5 minutes for the green check.
4. Open the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts", download Jarvis-APK, unzip it, and send app-debug.apk to your phone.
5. On the Tecno, open the APK and allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.
